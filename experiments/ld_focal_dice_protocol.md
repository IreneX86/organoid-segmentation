# LD small-droplet study: symmetric focal BCE + soft-Dice

**Protocol status (2026-10-14): fixed before candidate GPU fitting or candidate outer-test evaluation.** This is one exploratory, LD-only loss ablation against the frozen five-fold 2D U-Net baseline. The original TIFFs and Photoshop masks, `ld_antialias_midpoint_v1` derived 0/1 labels, grouped fold map, frozen baseline tag, all-36 production checkpoint, and existing `fluo_label` outputs remain unchanged. Record this file's SHA256 in every candidate run and decision. Changes to this protocol after candidate results must be versioned as a *new* experiment, never retroactively applied.

## Question and scope of prior evidence

The frozen LD baseline has 36-image OOF mean Dice **0.763693** (sample SD **0.106970**), IoU **0.628273**, precision **0.761894**, and recall **0.782860**, using each fold's inner-validation-selected threshold. It is the scientific comparator, not a model to retrain. The baseline report and prior single-factor component-uniform sampling and p99.95 normalization studies did not establish a reliable replacement.

The user separately confirmed **eight** visually selected, manually labelled small missed regions as LDs. They belong to the same 36-image development pool. Their near-zero baseline response in most cases suggests that merely lowering the deployment threshold is unlikely to recover them safely, but it does **not** prove that focal loss will help. Those eight selected points are discovery cases, **not** independent external validation or a special gate set. Do not reweight, relabel, crop-select, or threshold-tune specifically for their filenames or coordinates. The other inspected regions remain unconfirmed; no label is revised here.

The fixed hypothesis is that downweighting easy pixels within the existing BCE term may raise response to some difficult small labelled LD pixels without an unacceptable increase in false positives or loss of overall overlap. Class imbalance is severe; a focal loss can also worsen calibration or precision. Both possibilities are measured rather than assumed.

## The sole scientific change

Replace the baseline `mean(BCE(y,p)) + (1 - mean_per_image_soft_Dice(y,p))` by **symmetric, unweighted binary focal BCE with γ = 2, plus exactly the same soft-Dice term**:

```text
p_t = y*p + (1-y)*(1-p)
L = mean[ (1-p_t)^2 * {-y*log(p) - (1-y)*log(1-p)} ]
    + (1 - mean_per_image_soft_Dice(y,p))
```

Here `y∈{0,1}`; `p` is the sigmoid probability. Clip `p` by Keras epsilon only inside logarithms for numerical safety. Keep both loss terms at coefficient 1, use **no class alpha**, and do not scan γ, focal weights, or alternative losses after observing results. The existing registered `binary_focal_dice_loss` in `code/losses.py` implements this formula; its prior Mito use is *not* LD evidence or transfer learning. Candidate training should use an isolated LD entry point (`code/ld_focal_train.py`) so baseline source snapshots and hashes stay valid, and an isolated evaluation path (`code/ld_focal_evaluate.py`). New weights are initialized from scratch in every fold.

Everything else remains the frozen LD baseline: 2D U-Net base width 32 and four pooling stages; native-coordinate 512×512 crops and 1200×1200 full-image predictions; per-image p1–p99.8 normalization; 0.60 foreground-pixel-centred plus 0.40 uniform crop sampling; existing paired geometric and mild intensity augmentation; Adam 1e-4; batch size 4; 100 steps per epoch and maximum 100 epochs; seed 42 plus fold ID; full-image validation at fixed 0.50 for checkpoint, learning-rate reduction (patience 8) and early stopping (patience 20); 512-pixel sliding-window tiles with 128-pixel overlap and probability blending; and the existing 0.10–0.90 threshold grid in increments of 0.05. Do not combine focal loss with component-uniform sampling, p99.95 normalization, post-processing, a new architecture, or a new annotation rule. Leave Mito and Nuc untouched.

Use the frozen grouped [`ld_five_fold_map_v1.json`](results/ld_five_fold_map_v1.json) and the SHA256-verified **canonical** derived-label manifest at local `data/ld_binary_midpoint_v1/LD_output/manifest.json` (packaged as `/workspace/ld_study/manifest.json`). ## Checkpoint, threshold, and locked validation gate

Train **one** candidate per fold in a fresh output directory. Technical failures may be retried using identical science in a new directory; poor validation or test scores never justify a rerun. Archive source snapshot, exact command/configuration, split, environment, training history, checkpoint, full-image validation probabilities and masks, threshold grid, hashes, and a `selection.lock.json` before any candidate outer-test read. Select the checkpoint using that fold's full-image inner-validation mean Dice at **fixed 0.50**. Only then select its deployment threshold by maximum mean *inner-validation* per-image Dice on the unchanged grid. The frozen baseline checkpoint and threshold are not changed.

Compare candidate and matching frozen baseline on the **same fold's inner-validation images at fixed 0.50**. Eight-connected components of the derived binary GT mask with area **≤25 native pixels** are *semantic component proxies*, not confirmed separate lipid droplets. Count a component as detected when at least one candidate-foreground pixel overlaps it; use the same denominator for candidate and baseline. The candidate wins that fold's provisional validation-only gate only if **all** conditions below hold:

1. Mean per-image Dice is at least baseline mean Dice **−0.01** absolute.
2. The small-component any-overlap fraction is at least baseline **+0.10** absolute when baseline fraction is ≤0.90. If baseline fraction is >0.90, require **no loss of detected components** *and* candidate mean Dice at least baseline **+0.01**. If the fold contains zero such components, the gate is inconclusive and defaults to baseline. Record both hit numerators and the common GT denominator. The ceiling branch is fixed before candidate training: frozen baseline inner-validation hits were 7/7, 40/62, 21/39, 6/11, and 21/31 for folds 0–4 at fixed 0.50.
3. Mean per-image precision is at least baseline **−0.03** absolute.
4. No individual validation image loses more than **0.10** absolute Dice.

These are operational safety limits, not a significance test or a guarantee of biological object recall. Record every paired validation value, condition, baseline/candidate source and checkpoint hash, protocol hash, fold-map/label hashes, and `selected = candidate|baseline` in a write-once decision file. **Lock all five fold decisions before opening any candidate outer-test label.** Never revise the gate after seeing outer-test images, probabilities, metrics, or the selected eight discovery cases. The eight confirmed points may be shown only in an explicitly labelled post-hoc qualitative appendix after decisions are locked; their response cannot choose a threshold, fold, or production method.

## Outer evaluation and interpretation

After all five gates are locked, evaluate the fixed focal candidate on each outer fold exactly once, including folds where the gate retained baseline, so the raw candidate has a complete, same-split, 36-image paired OOF description. A fold-gated hybrid may also be reported separately as a **research procedure**, never as an already trained all-36 model. Calculate masks both at validation-selected threshold (primary) and fixed 0.50 (offline comparator) from the *same saved probabilities*; do not retrain for threshold comparisons. Do not pick the better threshold by test score.

Report per-image and per-fold Dice, IoU, precision, recall; 36-image mean/median/sample SD/minimum/maximum and paired candidate-minus-baseline changes; low-foreground images defined descriptively as **≤0.10% annotated foreground**; positive/negative paired changes; worst-case images; false-positive and false-negative pixel counts; and false positives >10 native pixels from GT. For ≤25-pixel GT semantic components, report pooled and per-fold any-overlap hits plus ≥50% pixel-coverage hits, always with denominators. Show whether component recall improves while pixel precision, recall, total FP, or worst-case Dice deteriorates. Component counts are annotation- and connectivity-dependent and must not be called instance-level recall. Include representative input/GT/probability/FP-FN overlays in a private local archive, without changing labels.

The primary decision is **whether the loss is worth further study**, based on the locked validation gate and the full pattern of descriptive paired OOF changes; a tiny mean Dice gain, one winning fold, or the eight selected point cases is insufficient. Regardless of results, this repeatedly inspected 36-image pool provides **exploratory internal evidence** only. Because rotated inner validation eventually includes images that are outer test in another fold, aggregating all five validation results to choose a universal production loss would also weaken a claim that the same OOF is untouched confirmation. No focal all-36 production model, replacement of the frozen baseline, or rewrite of existing predictions is authorized by this study alone. Reliable production promotion needs later independently annotated images with complete masks and predeclared small-LD/FP endpoints.
